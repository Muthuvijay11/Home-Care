COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions? We've Got Answers.

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What services do you provide?

We offer home services such as plumbing, carpentry, electrical, gardening, painting, and appliance care. We also provide ongoing property wellness plans and real estate support for purchase and resale, investment, and leasing.

What is included in the Home Harmony Plan?

The Home Harmony Plan covers everyday home services, from quick fixes to essential upkeep, across plumbing, carpentry, electrical, gardening, painting, and appliances.

What is the difference between the Standard, Signature, and Supreme plans?

Standard covers the essentials for everyday property care. Signature adds more thoughtful attention for extra peace of mind. Supreme is our premium approach to keeping your property at its best.

Can I book a one-time repair?

Yes. Choose the matching category in the booking form above, select the service you need, and describe the issue in the additional details field.

Do you help with buying and selling properties?

Yes. Our Property Hub covers purchase and resale, property investment, and leasing services, with guidance through each step of the process.

How do I book a service?

Fill in the Request a Service form with your name, phone number, the service category and type you need, and a preferred date if you have one. Our team will reach out within a few hours.

How much do your services cost?

Costs depend on the service and your property. Send us your request and our team will share the details that apply to your situation.
